Our Story

Lizzie's Kitchen started as a local family food stand in Ivory Park, driven by a simple goal: to feed our community affordable, delicious, high-quality street food. Over time, our loaded Kotas and flame-grilled meat platters became community favorites.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To prepare fresh, filling, and affordable street food staples with quick, friendly service to keep Ivory Park moving.

Our Vision

To lead digital innovation in local township takeaway food by offering rapid online pre-orders while maintaining our authentic local flavors.
